How you evaluate

(The student)

Check For:

  • Did the code do what was asked?
  • Did it follow all instructions?
  • Did it meet the rubric’s requirements?

Do Not Evaluate Based On:

  • How you would have written it
  • Whether it looks elegant
  • Whether it “almost” works

How you are evaluated

(As the teacher)

Your work is reviewed for:

  • Accuracy of pass/fail decisions
  • Alignment with the rubric
  • Consistency across similar cases
  • Clarity of explanations
Strong evaluators are consistent and easy to agree with.
